Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I register and post a car for sale?

Go back to the registration page, fill in your full name, email, password, and select your city. Once registered, click "Add Listing" to upload photos and details of your vehicle.

2. How can I check a car's VIN code / history?

Every listing on our platform includes a VIN code section. Always verify the VIN code before making a deposit or buying a used car.

3. Is it free to browse and contact sellers?

Yes! Browsing used cars and contacting sellers via phone or message is 100% free for all registered users.

4. Safety Tips for Buyers

Never send money in advance without inspecting the car in person. Always meet in a public place or at an authorized service center.
